### 1. The Problem: Traditional Venture Capital is Broken

For deep-tech ventures, capital is not the primary bottleneck—**engineering is**. Traditional VC models provide money but leave founders to solve immense technical challenges alone. This leads to slow development, high burn rates, and a high rate of failure for otherwise brilliant ideas. The model is inefficient and fails to de-risk the most critical phase: building the core product.

### 2. The Solution: A New Model of Venture Studio

**Eve Count is not a traditional VC. We are a technical co-founder.**

### 3. Our Model: The Engine

Our process is a true partnership designed for speed and technical excellence.

1.  **You Bring the Vision:** We start with an intensive, in-person **Foundry Session**. The founder provides the domain expertise; we provide the architects to forge a concrete technical roadmap.
2.  **We Provide the Engine:** We act as the dedicated technical team, building the MVP in stealth while the founder provides critical feedback to ensure perfect market alignment.
3.  **You Make the Decisions:** While we build the product, our partners handle the complex legal and corporate setup. The founder remains in control of all key decisions.
4.  **You Lead the Venture:** We launch together, but the founder leads the company. We position them with a market-ready product, initial users, and a compelling narrative for their seed round.
